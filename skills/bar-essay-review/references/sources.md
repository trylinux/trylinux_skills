## Grading-material structure
The rubric procedure in `mee-grading.md` and `mpt-grading.md` was derived by reviewing the structure of NCBE's July 2026 MEE analyses and MPT drafters' point sheets (purchased study aids). No question text, analysis text, or point-sheet text is included in this repo; only generalized observations about how the materials are organized and how their grader notes allocate credit.

## Notes
- Credit fractions (1.0 / 0.75 / 0.4 / 0.15 / 0) and the raw-score-to-band map in `SKILL.md` are this skill's heuristics, not NCBE scales.
- Band anchors in `SKILL.md` are the author's own wording, not official NCBE descriptors.
- Information current as of November 2026. Kentucky's last legacy UBE is February 2027; NextGen begins July 2027.
